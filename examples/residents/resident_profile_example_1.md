# Resident Profile

**Name**: Margaret Wilson
**Age:** 84  
**Care Needs:** Residential care  

**Background:**  
Margaret is a retired primary school teacher who has lived in the care home for two years. She enjoys reading, poetry, and classical music. She values routine and likes to remain as independent as possible.

**Health and mobility:**  
- Mild arthritis affecting her hands and knees  
- Uses a walking frame for short distances  
- No diagnosed cognitive impairment  

**Daily support needs:**  
- Minimal assistance with bathing and dressing  
- Support with managing medication  
- Encouragement to take part in light exercise  

**Personal interests:**  
- Reading newspapers and novels  
- Attending group quizzes and book discussions  
- Listening to music in the afternoons

### Resident Profile 2: George Patel

**Age:** 78  
**Care Needs:** Dementia care  

**Background:**  
George is a former shop owner who moved into the care home following a diagnosis of moderate Alzheimer’s disease. He sometimes becomes anxious in unfamiliar situations but responds well to reassurance and routine.

**Health and mobility:**  
- Moderate dementia affecting short-term memory  
- Physically mobile and able to walk independently  
- Occasional confusion and agitation, especially in the evenings  

**Daily support needs:**  
- Prompting and supervision with personal care  
- Support during mealtimes to ensure adequate nutrition  
- Structured daily routine to reduce anxiety  

**Personal interests:**  
- Listening to music from the 1960s  
- Talking about his past work and family  
- Participating in reminiscence therapy sessions

### Resident Profile 3: Ellen Roberts

**Age:** 91  
**Care Needs:** Nursing care  

**Background:**  
Ellen is a former seamstress who requires higher levels of care due to complex medical needs. She has lived in the care home for six months and prefers quiet, one-to-one interactions.

**Health and mobility:**  
- Limited mobility; uses a wheelchair  
- Chronic heart condition and osteoporosis  
- Hearing impairment  

**Daily support needs:**  
- Full assistance with personal care and transfers  
- Regular monitoring of vital signs and medication  
- Help with positioning for comfort and pressure relief  

**Personal interests:**  
- Knitting and sewing-related activities  
- One-to-one conversations with staff  
- Watching television and listening to the radio
