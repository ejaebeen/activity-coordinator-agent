# Chair-Based Exercise Session

**Description:**  
A gentle, structured exercise session designed for residents with limited mobility.

**How the activity is carried out:**  
- Led by an activities coordinator or physiotherapist in the morning  
- Residents sit in a circle on supportive chairs  
- Exercises include arm raises, ankle rotations, leg lifts, and light stretching  
- Familiar music may be played to encourage engagement  
- Session lasts approximately 20–30 minutes  

**Purpose and benefits:**  
- Maintains joint mobility and muscle strength  
- Improves circulation and reduces stiffness  
- Supports balance and helps reduce the risk of falls  
- Encourages social interaction and routine
