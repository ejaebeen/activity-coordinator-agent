# Assisted Meal and Lunchtime Support

**Description:**  
A supported mealtime for residents who require help with eating and drinking.

**How the activity is carried out:**  
- Care staff assist residents to the dining area and help with hand hygiene  
- Meals are adapted to individual dietary and medical needs  
- Staff provide support such as cutting food, prompting, or feeding if required  
- Fluid intake is monitored throughout the meal  
- Mealtimes are calm and unhurried  

**Purpose and benefits:**  
- Ensures residents receive adequate nutrition and hydration  
- Supports residents with swallowing difficulties or reduced independence  
- Preserves dignity and comfort  
- Provides a social and familiar daily routine
