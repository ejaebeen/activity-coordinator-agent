# Reminiscence Therapy Group

**Description:**  
A guided group activity where residents recall and share past experiences.

**How the activity is carried out:**  
- Small group sessions led by an activities coordinator  
- Use of prompts such as photographs, music, or familiar objects  
- Residents are encouraged but not pressured to participate  
- Sessions typically last 30–45 minutes in a calm environment  

**Purpose and benefits:**  
- Stimulates memory and cognitive function  
- Particularly beneficial for residents with dementia  
- Encourages communication and emotional wellbeing  
- Helps residents feel valued and connected
